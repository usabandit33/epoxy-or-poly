Common questions

Is epoxy the same as polyaspartic in Phoenix heat?

No. Public installer pages treat basic epoxy as cheaper up front and more likely to yellow or lift under Arizona sun and hot tires. Polyaspartic is described as UV-stable with a faster cure. Ask which chemistry is on the bid.

Does a flake floor mean it is polyaspartic?

No. Flake is a look. The chemistry under and over the flake can be epoxy, polyurea, polyaspartic, or a mix. Ask for the base and the topcoat by name.

Will a cheap epoxy last a Phoenix summer?

Public contractor pages in the Valley say ordinary epoxy can yellow, chalk, or peel where hot tires sit if it has no UV-stable topcoat. This site does not warrant any coating.

How long before I can park?

Ask the installer for walk-on and park times in writing. Public pages often list polyaspartic as next-day parking and epoxy as a multi-day cure. Times vary with product and weather.

Should I pick the cheapest square-foot number first?

A low number can be paint, a thin epoxy, or a system with no UV topcoat. Ask for chemistry, grind prep, and when you can park before comparing price.
